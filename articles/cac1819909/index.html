What CAC1819909 is not

CAC1819909 is a contractor license. It is not an equipment error code, a thermostat identifier, or a compressor part number. Confusion sometimes arises because HVAC systems display alphanumeric fault codes on their control boards, and a 9-character string can look similar to a manufacturer error code at a glance. A status code on a Carrier or Trane control board is a system-status output, not a contractor credential. The AC compressor and thermostat are physical components with their own model and serial numbers. Neither is related to the CAC licensing format. CAC1819909 belongs to the contractor, not to any piece of equipment, and it does not expire when a unit is replaced or a system is upgraded.
