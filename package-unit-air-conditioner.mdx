## Package unit air conditioner in Clearwater - Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration

A package unit air conditioner, called a self-contained AC system, is an all-in-one HVAC unit that houses the compressor assembly, evaporator coil, condenser coil and blower motor inside a single outdoor cabinet, delivering both cooling and heating to a building through one connected duct system. Every component that a split system spreads across two locations sits inside one enclosure, which is why the packaged AC unit is a common choice for buildings without interior mechanical space, including low-slope commercial rooftops, slab-foundation homes and light industrial facilities. Package unit air conditioners are a subtype of central air conditioning system, meaning they distribute conditioned air through supply and return ductwork the same way a conventional central system does. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration services these units throughout Clearwater and the surrounding area.

### What a package unit air conditioner is and how it differs from a split system

A package unit air conditioner is a central air conditioning system in which every refrigeration-cycle component occupies one self-contained outdoor cabinet. No indoor air handler is required. That single-cabinet design is the defining contrast with a split system air conditioner, where the compressor and condenser coil sit outside while the evaporator coil and blower live inside. Mini-split air conditioners and ductless mini-splits go further still, eliminating ductwork entirely and conditioning individual zones. The packaged AC unit retains the duct-based distribution model of central air while removing the need for any indoor mechanical equipment, which is why it appears on rooftops and beside slab foundations where interior space is limited.

The rooftop unit (RTU) is the most common commercial form of this product. Supply and return ducts connect through the roof deck or an exterior wall, and the entire refrigerant circuit stays outdoors, which simplifies refrigerant-line routing and reduces the risk of indoor refrigerant leaks. For [central air conditioning installation](https://poulincoolingacandrefrigeration.mintlify.app/central-air-conditioning-installation) on a building with no attic or basement, a packaged AC unit is often the most practical path.

### Measurable attributes: cooling capacity, SEER rating and cabinet dimensions

Package unit air conditioners are available in cooling capacities from 2 tons to 25 tons. That range covers services from a small residential slab home to a large retail or light-industrial building. Residential packaged units typically fall in the 2-to-5-ton range, while commercial package unit air conditioner sizing starts around 5 tons and scales upward based on square footage, occupancy load, and local climate data. Tonnage selection is critical. An oversized unit short-cycles and leaves humidity high, while an undersized unit runs continuously and never reaches setpoint.

SEER ratings on current packaged AC units run from 14 SEER to 20 SEER. Higher SEER ratings reduce monthly energy draw, but the payback period depends on run hours, which in a Clearwater climate are substantial. Cabinet dimensions vary by tonnage and manufacturer, so a site survey is necessary before ordering a replacement unit. Duct connections, electrical service size and pad or curb dimensions all have to match the new cabinet before installation begins. A licensed [air conditioning contractor](https://poulincoolingacandrefrigeration.mintlify.app/air-conditioning-contractor) measures all three before specifying a replacement.

### How a package unit air conditioner fits into the broader HVAC system

A package unit air conditioner is installed by an AC installation service and connects to the building's existing duct network through supply and return plenums. Because the entire refrigerant circuit is self-contained, the duct system is the only interface between the unit and the building interior. That makes duct condition critical: leaky or undersized ductwork limits airflow regardless of how well the packaged AC unit itself performs. On new construction, the duct layout is designed around the unit's static pressure requirements from the start. For [new construction AC installation](https://poulincoolingacandrefrigeration.mintlify.app/new-construction-ac-installation), specifying the correct unit tonnage and duct sizing together prevents airflow problems before the building is occupied.

The packaged AC unit integrates with the building's thermostat and electrical panel. Most residential units run on 240-volt single-phase power. Larger commercial rooftop units require three-phase service. Control wiring runs from the thermostat to a low-voltage terminal board inside the cabinet. Modern units accept communicating thermostats and building automation inputs, though the base refrigeration cycle operates independently of those controls.

### Common problems and signs that a package unit air conditioner needs repair

Package unit air conditioner repair addresses a predictable set of failure points because all components share one enclosure and one outdoor environment. Clearwater's heat, humidity and salt air accelerate corrosion on condenser coil fins and electrical terminals, which are the two most common causes of reduced capacity and nuisance tripping. The signs below indicate the unit needs a technician.

- **Warm air from supply registers:** Warm air at the registers points to a refrigerant leak, a failed compressor or a dirty evaporator coil blocking heat transfer.

- **Short cycling:** Short cycling, where the unit starts and stops every few minutes, often traces to a low refrigerant charge, a faulty high-pressure switch or an oversized unit relative to the load.

- **Unusual noise from the cabinet:** Rattling or grinding from the outdoor cabinet signals a loose panel, a failing blower motor bearing or debris inside the condenser section.

- **High humidity indoors:** High indoor humidity despite normal thermostat readings means the evaporator coil is not removing latent heat, usually because airflow across it is restricted or the refrigerant charge is low.

- **Ice on the cabinet or coil:** Ice formation on a packaged AC unit indicates restricted airflow, a low refrigerant charge or a malfunctioning metering device.

- **Tripped breaker or blown fuse:** Repeated electrical faults point to a compressor drawing locked-rotor amperage, a grounded motor winding or a failing capacitor.

Accurate diagnosis requires measuring supply and return air temperatures, checking static pressure across the coil, reading refrigerant pressures on both the high and low side and testing electrical components under load. [System diagnostics](https://poulincoolingacandrefrigeration.mintlify.app/system-diagnostics) performed by a licensed technician identify the root cause rather than treating symptoms, which prevents repeat failures on the same component.

### Where package unit air conditioners apply and when to replace one

Package unit air conditioners are common equipment for slab-foundation homes, manufactured housing, strip malls, retail storefronts, schools, and light industrial buildings. Any structure lacking a mechanical room, basement, or attic large enough for an air handler is a natural fit. Rooftop units dominate commercial applications. They keep all mechanical equipment off the occupied floor, simplify service access, and free interior square footage for productive use.

The decision to replace a package unit AC rather than repair it depends on age, refrigerant type, and repair cost relative to replacement cost. R-22 refrigerant is a key factor. Units still operating on R-22 face supply constraints because R-22 production ended under federal phaseout rules, making refrigerant-related repairs increasingly expensive. A unit older than 15 years with a compressor failure is typically a replacement candidate. When the repair cost exceeds roughly half the installed cost of a new unit, replacement delivers better long-term value, particularly given the efficiency gains from moving to a current 16-SEER or higher model from a 10-SEER legacy unit.
