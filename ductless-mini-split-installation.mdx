### Ductless mini-split installation is

Ductless mini-split installation is a form of air conditioning installation that mounts an outdoor condenser on a pad or wall bracket, runs a refrigerant line set and low-voltage control wiring through a 3-inch wall penetration, and secures one or more indoor air handlers to interior walls or ceilings. The system is a type of air conditioning installation, sitting under the broader category of HVAC installation services, and it contrasts directly with central ducted AC installation, which routes conditioned air through a network of supply and return ducts. ### Measurable attributes of a ductless mini-split system

Ductless mini-split installation covers a wide range of system sizes, and matching the right attributes to the space is the core technical task. The attributes below define what a properly specified system looks like.

- **Cooling capacity:** Cooling capacity runs from 6,000 BTU/hr for a small bedroom to 60,000 BTU/hr for a large open-plan space, and undersizing a unit by even one capacity tier leaves the room unable to reach setpoint on a hot Clearwater afternoon.

- **Number of indoor air handler zones:** A single-zone system serves one room; multi-zone ductless mini-split installation connects 2 to 8 indoor air handlers to one outdoor condenser, letting each zone hold a different temperature simultaneously.

- **SEER2 efficiency rating:** SEER2 ratings for mini-splits range from 16 to 42. Requirements can vary and should be confirmed with the local building authority.

- **Electrical circuit requirement:** Requirements for dedicated circuits can vary and should be confirmed with the local building authority.

- **Number of indoor units:** A single outdoor condenser supports 1 to 5 or more indoor units, so a homeowner adding a guest suite, a garage workshop and a sunroom can serve all three from one outdoor installation point.

- **Refrigerant charge:** The refrigerant line set must be vacuumed and charged to the manufacturer's specified weight in pounds before the system starts; an incorrect charge is the leading cause of poor performance after installation.

### Ductless mini-split installation relates to the broader HVAC system

Ductless mini-split installation requires three supporting components to reach full commissioning: a refrigerant line set, an electrical disconnect, and a wall penetration. The refrigerant line set carries the charge between the outdoor condenser and each indoor air handler; the electrical disconnect provides a local shutoff point; and the wall penetration, sealed against moisture and pests, is the physical path that makes the ductless design possible. ### Steps in a professional ductless mini-split installation

Professional ductless mini-split installation follows 6 ordered steps, and skipping or rushing any one of them produces a system that underperforms or fails early.

- **Site assessment:** Site assessment confirms room square footage, wall construction, panel capacity and the path for the line set before any equipment is ordered.

- **Outdoor condenser mounting:** Outdoor condenser mounting sets the unit on a concrete pad or wall bracket at the correct height and clearance from obstructions, so airflow across the coil stays unrestricted.

- **Wall penetration:** Wall penetration drills the 3-inch sleeve, angles it slightly downward to drain condensate, and seals it against Florida's humidity and insects.

- **Indoor air handler bracketing:** Indoor air handler bracketing secures the wall plate level and at the height specified by the manufacturer, typically 6 to 8 inches from the ceiling, for even air distribution.

- **Refrigerant line set connection:** Refrigerant line set connection runs insulated copper lines from the indoor unit to the outdoor condenser, flares the fittings, and torques them to spec to prevent leaks.

- **Vacuuming and commissioning:** Vacuuming refrigerant lines pulls the system to a deep vacuum to remove moisture and air, then the technician charges the system to the manufacturer's specified refrigerant weight and verifies operation across all modes.
