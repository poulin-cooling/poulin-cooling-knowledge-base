## EPA Refrigerant Management Regulations in Clearwater

EPA Refrigerant Management Regulations, called Refrigerant Compliance Rules, are a category of Federal Environmental Regulations governing how refrigerants are purchased, handled, recovered, recycled, and disposed of during HVAC service work, commercial refrigeration maintenance, and new equipment installation. These rules apply to hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs), and hydrochlorofluorocarbons (HCFCs) used in cooling systems across residential, commercial, and industrial settings. ### What non-compliance looks like and what it opposes

Non-compliant AC service includes actions that violate federal environmental rules such as improper refrigerant handling. These are the direct antonyms of what the regulations require. Unregulated refrigerant release is the specific harm the rules are designed to prevent. Refrigerants with high GWP values trap heat in the atmosphere at rates hundreds to thousands of times greater than carbon dioxide, and CFCs are a significant contributor to stratospheric ozone depletion.
