### Measurable attributes: frequency, duration and filter replacement interval

Whole house air purifier maintenance runs on a defined service calendar with specific, measurable targets at each visit. The attributes below set the baseline for a properly maintained central filtration system. Technicians often combine this work with [AC maintenance](https://poulincoolingacandrefrigeration.mintlify.app/ac-maintenance) visits to address related components in one appointment.

- **Frequency:** Frequency for a full central air purifier service is every 6 to 12 months, with bi-annual visits recommended for homes with pets, high occupancy or known allergy concerns.

- **Filter replacement interval:** Filter replacement interval ranges from 3 to 12 months depending on filter type. Thinner 1-inch media typically needs replacement every 3 months, while deep 4-to-5-inch media cartridges last up to 12 months.

- **Duration:** Duration of a standard maintenance visit is 1 to 3 hours, covering inspection, media swap, sanitization and airflow verification.

- **Pressure differential:** Pressure differential across the filter, measured in inches of water column, is the primary indicator of loading. A reading above the manufacturer's threshold means the media is saturated and airflow is restricted.

- **Airflow capacity:** Airflow capacity, measured in CFM, is verified after service to confirm the blower is delivering the volume the system was sized for; a post-service reading below the rated CFM points to a duct restriction or blower fault.

- **Operating noise level:** Operating noise level, measured in dB, rises when the blower motor strains against a clogged filter. A noticeable increase between visits is a reliable early sign that the filter replacement interval has been exceeded.
