### What new construction AC installation is

New Construction AC Installation consists of the design, placement, and commissioning of a cooling system inside an unoccupied building, with tasks divided across two distinct building phases known as rough-in and trim-out. Rough-in includes ductwork layout, line set runs, and electrical stub-outs finished before drywall closes the walls. Trim-out includes equipment mounting, refrigerant charging, and thermostat wiring after the interior reaches a finished state. Split-systems and mini-splits represent the most common system types placed in new residential construction, although packaged units appear on slab-on-grade projects where attic space is limited. Open walls during rough-in allow a contractor to route ducts and line sets along optimal paths instead of fishing through finished cavities. ### New construction HVAC installation checklist: phases and coordination points

A new construction HVAC installation checklist identifies the tasks and sign-offs required at each stage of the build. Timing remains critical. The rough-in phase must reach completion and pass inspection before insulation and drywall installation proceeds, therefore the checklist places the most time-sensitive items at the beginning. Trim-out and commissioning take place near the end of construction once interior finishes are complete; these steps include the refrigerant charge, airflow balancing, and thermostat programming. Coordination with general contractors in Clearwater supports adherence to each phase schedule and prevents inspection delays.

- **Pre-construction load calculation:** Load calculation determines system capacity in tons before any equipment is ordered or ductwork is laid out.

- **Permit pull:** Permit pull requirements can vary and should be confirmed with the local building authority before rough-in begins.

- **Rough-in ductwork layout:** Rough-in ductwork layout sets supply and return runs through open framing, sized to the load calculation.

- **Refrigerant line set placement:** Refrigerant line set placement routes copper lines between the air handler and condenser pad before walls close.

- **Electrical rough-in coordination:** Electrical rough-in coordination confirms that disconnect boxes and circuit breakers are in place before the HVAC crew returns for trim-out.

- **Equipment pad and air handler setting:** Equipment pad and air handler setting positions the outdoor condenser and indoor unit at trim-out, after interior finishes are complete.

- **Refrigerant charge and startup:** Refrigerant charge and startup commissions the system, verifies pressures and confirms airflow at every register.

- **Final inspection sign-off:** Final inspection requirements can vary and should be confirmed with the local building authority to confirm the installed system meets local mechanical code before occupancy.
