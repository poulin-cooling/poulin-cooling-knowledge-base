## Central air conditioning installation in Clearwater - Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration

Central air conditioning installation. Called whole-house AC setup. Is a type of HVAC Installation Service that places a complete cooling system inside a building. It connects an outdoor condenser unit, an indoor air handler, a refrigerant line set, and a ductwork system to deliver conditioned air to every room. The work covers site assessment, equipment selection, ductwork connection or fabrication, electrical supply hookup, refrigerant line charging, and a final performance test before the system runs. Most residential installs in Clearwater take 4 to 12 hours for a straightforward replacement. New ductwork extends that timeline to up to 3 days.

### What central air conditioning installation is

Central air conditioning installation sets up a split-system or packaged cooling unit to serve an entire structure through a shared duct network. A split-system places the compressor and condenser outdoors and the air handler indoors. Packaged units differ. They house all components in one outdoor cabinet and connect directly to the duct system. Either configuration requires a dedicated electrical circuit, a properly sized refrigerant line set, and a duct system capable of moving the rated airflow without excessive static pressure. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration handles both configurations for homes and light commercial buildings in Clearwater.

### Measurable attributes: capacity, SEER rating and installation duration

Central AC installation is sized by two key measures: cooling capacity in tons and efficiency in SEER2. Residential systems range from 1.5 to 5 tons. The correct size depends on the square footage, ceiling height, insulation level, and window area of the building. Efficiency ratings run from 13 to 26 SEER2, with higher numbers delivering lower monthly energy use. Florida's climate and utility rates make a mid-to-high SEER2 unit a cost-effective consideration for many homeowners over the life of the equipment.

Installation duration is a separate measurable attribute. A central AC install on an existing duct system typically takes 4 to 8 hours. Adding or replacing ductwork extends the job to 2 to 3 days. Poulin Cooling schedules the work so the home is without cooling for the shortest practical window, which matters in Clearwater's heat.

### How central AC installation relates to the broader HVAC system

Central air conditioning installation sits inside the larger category of HVAC Installation Service and depends on three supporting systems: the ductwork system, the electrical supply and the refrigerant line set. Each of those systems must meet the load the new equipment places on it. Undersized ductwork raises static pressure and shortens equipment life; an undersized electrical circuit trips breakers and voids warranties; an improperly charged refrigerant line set reduces capacity and raises operating cost. A licensed [air conditioning contractor](https://poulincoolingacandrefrigeration.mintlify.app/air-conditioning-contractor) sizes and verifies all three before the system is commissioned.

Central AC installation connects forward to the finished system it produces: a functional central air conditioning system that controls temperature and humidity across every conditioned space. That finished system is what distinguishes a whole-house AC setup from a room-by-room solution. New construction projects follow a slightly different sequence, where ductwork is roughed in before walls close; that workflow is covered in detail under [new construction AC installation](https://poulincoolingacandrefrigeration.mintlify.app/new-construction-ac-installation).

### Central air conditioning installation vs. Alternatives and contrasts

Central air conditioning installation is not the same as ductless mini-split installation, portable AC unit setup or AC system repair. A ductless mini-split installation serves individual zones without a duct network and suits additions, garages or buildings where running ducts is impractical. A portable AC unit setup requires no permanent work and cools one room at a time, with no connection to a central distribution system. AC system repair addresses faults in an existing installation rather than placing new equipment. When an existing system is beyond repair or has reached the end of its service life, the correct path is [AC unit replacement](https://poulincoolingacandrefrigeration.mintlify.app/ac-unit-replacement), which shares many steps with a new central AC install but starts from a removal rather than a blank slate.

Central air conditioning removal is the direct antonym: it takes out an installed system rather than putting one in, and it is typically the first step of a replacement job.

### Common signs a new central air conditioning installation is needed

A new central air conditioning installation becomes necessary when repair costs approach or exceed the value of the existing equipment, when the system can no longer hold the set temperature on a 90-degree Clearwater afternoon or when the unit is more than 15 years old and running on a refrigerant type that is no longer widely available. Uneven cooling across rooms, humidity that stays above 60 percent indoors despite the system running and electric bills that climb year over year without a change in usage are the three most common signals homeowners notice before calling for a replacement install. The cost of central air conditioning installation is a one-time expense that can result in lower monthly energy costs when the new unit carries a significantly higher SEER2 rating than the one it replaces.
