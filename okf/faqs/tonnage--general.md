# what is tonnage

Tonnage is the unit of measurement that defines an air conditioning system's cooling capacity. One ton equals 12,000 BTU of heat removed per hour. Residential systems typically range from 1.5 to 5 tons; commercial systems extend to 50 tons or more. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration sizes every system to the correct tonnage for each specific structure it serves in Clearwater, FL, using a load calculation before any equipment is selected.
