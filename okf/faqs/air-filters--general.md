# what is air filters

Air filters are devices that remove particulate matter from an HVAC air stream, protecting indoor air quality and mechanical components. Rated on a MERV scale of 1 to 20, available in 1- to 6-inch thicknesses, they trap dust, pollen, mold spores, and dander before air reaches the blower and coil.
