# how often refrigerant ac

Refrigerant does not need routine replacement — Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration explains that refrigerant does not deplete through normal operation. A system requiring recharge every one to two seasons has an active leak needing detection and repair first. Poulin Cooling serves Clearwater, FL, using electronic leak detectors and UV dye to locate leaks before adding refrigerant, ensuring the system holds its correct charge long-term.
