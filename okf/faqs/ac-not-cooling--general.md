# why is ac not cooling

AC not cooling occurs when an air conditioner runs but fails to lower indoor temperature to the thermostat set point. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration identifies three primary causes: inadequate refrigerant levels (often from a coolant leak), blocked airflow causing a frozen evaporator coil, and faulty compressor operation. Clogged drain lines triggering a float switch shutoff are a secondary cause. Poulin Cooling serves Clearwater, FL residents and businesses diagnosing these conditions.
