# why is air filter

A dirty air filter is a clogged filter media condition caused by accumulated dust, debris, and airborne particles restricting airflow through an HVAC system. In Clearwater's humid climate, year-round operation and coastal particulates accelerate buildup. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration addresses dirty air filter conditions during HVAC maintenance visits, inspecting for gray-to-black discoloration and moderate-to-heavy particle accumulation across residential and commercial systems.
