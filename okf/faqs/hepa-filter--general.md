# why hepa filter

True HEPA media meets a laboratory test standard for capturing particles as small as 0.3 microns at 99.97% efficiency, addressing airborne contaminants, allergens, and biological particles that standard filters miss. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration offers indoor-air-quality and filtration options — which may include higher-efficiency media or dedicated air-cleaning equipment — appropriate to each system's available airflow and static-pressure capacity. In Clearwater's coastal climate, salt particulates and elevated pollen loads can make higher-efficiency filtration a priority for indoor air quality.
