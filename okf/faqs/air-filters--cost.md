# how much air filters

Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration does not publish pricing for air filters. Filter costs vary by MERV rating (scale 1–20), thickness (1–6 inches), and replacement frequency — for example, a 1-inch filter in a pet household needs replacement every 30 days, while a 6-inch media filter lasts up to 12 months. Contact Poulin Cooling at (727) 252-9602 for specific filter recommendations and pricing for your Clearwater system.
