# what is ventilation

Ventilation is the intentional introduction and removal of air from a space, measured in air changes per hour (ACH), cubic feet per minute (CFM), and static pressure in inches of water column. Ventilation controls air intake, exhaust, and distribution to replace stale or contaminated indoor air with fresh, conditioned air.
