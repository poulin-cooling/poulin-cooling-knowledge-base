# how much ventilation is needed

Ventilation requirements vary by space type. Residential living areas need at least 0.35 ACH or 15 CFM per occupant; bathrooms require 50 CFM exhaust and kitchens 100 CFM exhaust. Commercial kitchens need 6–15 ACH, server rooms 20–60 ACH, and retail/office spaces 0.06 CFM per square foot minimum. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration in Clearwater, FL measures and verifies these values using static pressure testing and CFM readings at each register.
