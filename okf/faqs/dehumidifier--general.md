# what is dehumidifier

A dehumidifier is an air treatment appliance that removes excess moisture from indoor air by drawing humid air across a cold coil, condensing water vapor, and collecting the extracted water in a tank. Residential units remove 30 to 70 pints of moisture per day. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration installs, services, and maintains dehumidifiers for residential and commercial properties throughout Clearwater, FL, sizing each unit to the specific moisture load of the space.
