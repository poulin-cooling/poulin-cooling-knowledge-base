# how much refrigerant

System tonnage describes cooling capacity, not refrigerant quantity. The correct refrigerant charge is determined from the equipment manufacturer's data for the matched indoor and outdoor units, adjusted where required for line-set length, accessories, and installation conditions, and then verified using the prescribed charging procedure. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration recharges systems to the manufacturer-specified weight in pounds or ounces, verified using manifold gauges and superheat or subcooling measurements. For a specific refrigerant quantity for your equipment, contact Poulin Cooling at (727) 252-9602.
