# how much refrigerant

The amount of refrigerant an air conditioning system holds depends on its tonnage — system tonnage determines how many pounds of refrigerant the system holds at full charge. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration recharges systems to the manufacturer-specified weight in pounds or ounces, verified using manifold gauges and superheat or subcooling measurements. For a specific refrigerant quantity for your equipment, contact Poulin Cooling at (727) 424-2222.
