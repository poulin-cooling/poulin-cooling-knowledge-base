# how long air filter

Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration's reference material does not specify a set replacement interval for air filters. What is documented is that in Clearwater's humid, coastal climate, filter media reaches moderate to heavy particle accumulation faster than in drier inland climates—making regular inspection important. Poulin Cooling technicians assess filter condition during scheduled HVAC maintenance visits and evaluate both discoloration and particle accumulation level.
