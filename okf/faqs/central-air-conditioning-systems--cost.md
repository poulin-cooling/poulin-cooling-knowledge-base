# how much air conditioning

Central air conditioning systems handled by Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration range from 12,000 BTU per hour for smaller residential spaces up to 60,000+ BTU per hour for large homes and light commercial buildings. Energy efficiency ratings span 13 to 26 SEER across available equipment. Current federal efficiency requirements use SEER2 and EER2 ratings. Because the applicable minimum depends on system type, capacity, equipment combination, manufacturing date, and regional rules, Poulin Cooling verifies compliance for the specific matched system being installed. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration evaluates each Clearwater property to match cooling capacity to the building's specific load.
