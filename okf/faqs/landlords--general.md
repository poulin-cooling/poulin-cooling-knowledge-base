# what is landlords

Landlords are property owners who lease residential or commercial spaces to tenants, and responsibility for maintaining HVAC systems during the lease term can depend on the lease and any applicable local property-maintenance codes rather than a blanket statewide rule. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration serves landlords across Clearwater with scheduled maintenance, emergency repair, and system replacement services scaled to portfolio size — from single-unit investors to large multi-property operators managing residential, multi-unit, and mixed-use commercial properties.
