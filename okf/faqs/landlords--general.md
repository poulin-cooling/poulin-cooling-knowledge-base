# what is landlords

Landlords are property owners who lease residential or commercial spaces to tenants and, under Florida law, are required to maintain functional HVAC systems throughout the lease term. Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration serves landlords across Clearwater with scheduled maintenance, emergency repair, and system replacement services scaled to portfolio size — from single-unit investors to large multi-property operators managing residential, multi-unit, and mixed-use commercial properties.
