# what is refrigerant

Refrigerant is a chemical compound that absorbs and releases heat inside a closed-loop HVAC system, enabling air conditioning equipment to cool indoor spaces. It circulates between an evaporator coil, where it absorbs indoor heat as a gas, and a condenser coil, where it releases that heat outdoors as a liquid.
