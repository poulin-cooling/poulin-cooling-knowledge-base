Glossary — Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration

Promotional and educational coverage of Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration. This site is not operated by Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration.

  • Central Air Conditioning Installation: Central air conditioning installation.
  • New Construction AC Installation: New Construction AC Installation, called Construction Phase HVAC Installation, is a type of HVAC Installation Service performed during active building construction before a structure is occupied.
  • AC System Replacement: AC System Replacement, called Air Conditioner Replacement, is the full removal of an existing cooling unit and the installation of a new system in its place.
  • Ductless Mini-Split Installation: Ductless mini-split installation, called mini-split setup or zoned AC installation, is a type of air conditioning installation that connects an outdoor compressor unit to one or more indoor air handlers without any ductwork, delivering targeted cooling to individual rooms, additions, garages, sunrooms, converted attics and server spaces through a small wall penetration that carries refrigerant lines and electrical wiring.
  • Multi-Zone Mini-Split System: A multi-zone mini-split system, called a multi-zone ductless system, connects one outdoor condenser unit to between two and eight individual indoor air handlers.
  • Split System Air Conditioner: A split system air conditioner, called a split AC unit, separates the cooling process across two distinct units.
  • Package Unit Air Conditioner: A package unit air conditioner, called a self-contained AC system, is an all-in-one HVAC unit that houses the compressor assembly, evaporator coil, condenser coil and blower motor inside a single outdoor cabinet, delivering both cooling and heating to a building through one connected duct system.
  • High-Efficiency Air Conditioner: A high-efficiency air conditioner.
  • Whole-Home Air Filtration System: A whole-home air filtration system, called a Centralized Air Filtration Unit, is a type of Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) Equipment integrated directly into residential HVAC ductwork.
  • UV Light for HVAC Systems: UV light for HVAC systems.
  • Air Distribution System: An air distribution system, or duct system, is the network of ducts, vents, plenums, and air handler components that moves conditioned air from a central AC unit into every occupied zone of a building.
  • Plenum: A plenum, called an air plenum chamber, is a pressurized enclosure that receives conditioned air directly from an air handler and distributes it into the supply duct system throughout a building.
  • Outdoor Unit Mounting Pad: An outdoor unit mounting pad, called an HVAC pad, is a rigid base platform placed beneath an AC condenser unit.
  • Concrete Pad: A concrete pad, called a condenser pad, is a flat, reinforced concrete slab that is a stable equipment foundation for outdoor AC condensers, heat pump units and other heavy mechanical equipment placed at ground level.
  • Wall Bracket Mount: A wall bracket mount, called an AC wall mount or condenser wall bracket, is a steel fixing bracket that secures an outdoor AC condenser unit to a wall surface, keeping the unit elevated, level and stable.
  • Electrical Disconnect Box: An electrical disconnect box, called an AC disconnect, is a type of electrical safety equipment mounted near an outdoor condenser unit.
  • Dedicated Circuit: A dedicated circuit, called a dedicated branch circuit, is a type of electrical circuit reserved exclusively for one high-draw appliance or system.
  • Aging HVAC Equipment: Aging HVAC equipment.
  • Uneven Home Cooling: Uneven home cooling, called temperature imbalance, is a type of AC system performance problem where different areas of a home maintain inconsistent temperatures, producing hot and cold spots across rooms, floors, hallways and living spaces that a properly functioning system should hold at a uniform level.
  • High Indoor Humidity: High indoor humidity, or damp indoor air, is a type of indoor environmental condition in which the relative humidity level inside a building rises above 60% RH, creating an environment where airborne water vapor, condensation moisture load and latent heat gain accumulate faster than the building's ventilation and cooling systems can remove them.
  • Old AC Unit Removal: Old AC unit removal, called AC unit disposal or old AC haul-away, is the process of safely disconnecting, dismantling and hauling away an existing air conditioning unit before a new system goes in, covering refrigerant recovery, electrical disconnection and physical removal of equipment that typically weighs between 50 and 300 lbs.
  • Florida AC Contractor License: A Florida AC Contractor License, called a CAC License Florida, is the official state authorization permitting a contractor to legally perform air conditioning contracting services in Florida.
  • FL Certified Air Conditioning Contractor License: The FL Certified Air Conditioning Contractor License, commonly called the CAC License, is a Florida state credential authorizing contractors to install, service, repair and maintain air conditioning systems, refrigeration equipment, ventilation systems and related mechanical components anywhere in the state.
  • Permit: A permit.
  • Manufacturer's AC System Warranty: A manufacturer's AC system warranty, called an HVAC system warranty or factory AC warranty, is a written legal guarantee issued by the equipment manufacturer that covers defects in AC system components for a defined period following installation, including the compressor, refrigerant coils, heat exchanger, electrical controls and other covered parts.
  • Extended Labor Warranty: An extended labor warranty, called a service contract or Labor Protection Plan, is a post-installation coverage term that pays labor costs for qualifying repair callbacks after the standard installation period ends.
  • Contractor AC Installation Warranty: A contractor AC installation warranty, called a contractor labor guarantee, is a written promise from the installing contractor to repair or replace faulty workmanship within a defined period after a new AC system goes in.
  • Equipment Sizing: Equipment sizing, called load calculation or AC sizing, is the engineering process of calculating the exact cooling and heating capacity a building requires so the selected AC unit matches the actual thermal demand of the space, accounting for square footage, insulation levels, window area, local climate, occupancy and internal heat sources.
  • AC System Load Calculation: AC System Load Calculation, called Cooling Load Calculation, is an engineering process that determines the precise cooling capacity an AC system must deliver for a given space.
  • Energy Star Certified AC Units: Energy Star Certified AC Units, called high-efficiency AC units, are air conditioning systems that meet the EPA's defined efficiency thresholds for reduced energy consumption, lower operating costs and measurably higher performance than standard equipment, covering central split systems, packaged units, mini-splits and heat pump configurations across a wide range of cooling capacities.
  • AHRI Certified AC Equipment: AHRI Certified AC Equipment, called AHRI-rated equipment, is a product subtype within the broader HVAC Equipment Certification category.
  • Ductless Mini-Split Air Conditioner: A ductless mini-split air conditioner consists of three components: an indoor air handler, an outdoor condenser unit, and a refrigerant line set connecting the two.
  • Packaged Unit Repair: Packaged unit repair, called RTU Repair, is the diagnosis and restoration of single-cabinet air conditioning systems where the compressor, condenser coil, evaporator coil and blower fan all live inside one self-contained housing, typically mounted on a rooftop or a concrete pad beside a building.
  • TXV Valve: A TXV valve, or Thermostatic Expansion Valve, is a refrigerant metering device that controls the amount of liquid refrigerant entering the evaporator coil by responding to suction-line temperature and pressure in real time.
  • Pressure Testing: Pressure testing, called a System Pressure Check, is a leak detection process that introduces pressurized gas into a sealed refrigerant system and monitors the system for pressure loss, identifying the location and severity of leaks before refrigerant is charged or a system is returned to normal operation.
  • Leak Detection: Leak detection, called Refrigerant Leak Diagnostics, is the process of locating breaches in a sealed AC system where refrigerant, nitrogen, or other pressurized fluids escape into the surrounding environment, covering the inspection of refrigerant lines, coil connections, valve fittings, compressor ports and condenser joints to pinpoint the exact source of loss.
  • AC Electrical Wiring: AC electrical wiring, called HVAC wiring, is the network of conductors, insulation jackets, termination lugs, connectors and circuit breakers that carries line-voltage power from a home's electrical panel to the air conditioning unit and distributes that power through every stage of the cooling system.
  • Electrical Connections: Electrical connections, called circuit connections, are junctures that allow electric current to flow between two or more conductors inside an electrical system.
  • Dirty Condenser Coils: Dirty condenser coils, called fouled condenser coils, are coil surfaces coated with accumulated dirt, dust, debris, pollen, grass clippings, and biological growth.
  • AC Unit Not Turning On: AC unit not turning on, called AC not powering on, is a type of AC unit problem where the air conditioning system fails to initiate any operational functions when commanded, covering failures at the power supply, thermostat command signal, control voltage circuit, compressor activation stage and system indicator response.
  • AC Freezing Up: AC Freezing Up, called Air Conditioner Freeze or a frozen AC coil, is a type of Air Conditioner Problem where ice forms on the evaporator coil inside the air handler.
  • AC Water Leak: An AC water leak, called an AC condensation leak, is unintended water escaping from an air conditioning system.
  • Noisy AC Unit: A noisy AC unit, called a loud air conditioner, describes a system that emits unusual or excessive sound during operation.
  • Low AC Airflow: Low AC airflow, called Insufficient Airflow, is a condition where an air conditioning unit delivers less air volume than its manufacturer's specification requires.
  • High AC Energy Consumption: High AC energy consumption, called inefficient AC operation, is a measurable condition.
  • Air Flow Optimization: Air flow performance, called Airflow Tuning, is the adjustment of ducts, registers, dampers and blower speed to achieve proper air distribution and velocity throughout a conditioned space, covering supply and return duct balancing, static pressure measurement, register calibration, blower motor speed setting and temperature differential verification across the evaporator coil.
  • Humidity Control: Humidity control, called dehumidification, is the regulation of moisture content in indoor air, covering the measurement of relative humidity, the adjustment of dew point, the removal of excess water vapor from living spaces and commercial buildings, the prevention of mold growth, and the maintenance of air quality at levels that protect both occupants and building materials.
  • Noise Reduction: Noise reduction, called sound dampening, is the deliberate lowering of sound and vibration produced by air conditioning equipment.
  • AC Inspection: AC inspection, called an HVAC inspection or AC check-up, is a systematic examination of an air conditioning unit.
  • Warranty Coverage for AC Repair: Warranty coverage for AC repair, called AC Repair Warranty Terms, is a formal service guarantee that promises to remedy defects in parts, labor, or both within a defined period after a repair is completed, covering conditions such as recurring refrigerant leaks, failed compressor components, faulty electrical connections, defective replacement parts and workmanship errors that cause the same fault to return.
  • Energy Efficiency Standards: Energy efficiency standards, called an Efficiency Directive, are rules set by regulatory bodies to define the minimum energy performance that heating, cooling and refrigeration equipment must meet before it can be sold or installed, covering SEER ratings for air conditioners, HSPF ratings for heat pumps, EER ratings for packaged units and AFUE ratings for furnaces, along with the testing protocols and compliance timelines that govern each product category.
  • AC Manufacturer Warranty: An AC Manufacturer Warranty, called a Factory Warranty or Manufacturer's Warranty, is a type of Warranty issued directly by the equipment manufacturer that covers the cost of repairing or replacing defective parts in an air conditioning system for a defined period after installation, typically spanning compressors, coils, electrical components, heat exchangers and refrigerant-circuit parts.
  • EPA Certification for Refrigerant Handling: EPA Certification for Refrigerant Handling, called Section 608 Certification, is a professional credential that authorizes individuals to legally purchase, recover, recycle, and dispose of refrigerants used in stationary air conditioning and refrigeration equipment.
  • CAC1819909: CAC1819909 is the Florida state-issued Air Conditioning License held by Poulin Cooling A/C and Refrigeration.
