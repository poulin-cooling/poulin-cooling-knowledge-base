### What landlords are and how they operate in Clearwater

Landlords own property and lease residential or commercial spaces to tenants in return for recurring rental income. Their primary duties include keeping living conditions habitable, administering tenant agreements, and ensuring mechanical systems remain operational, with exact requirements set by the lease and applicable local codes. Florida Statute §83.51 does not create a statewide mandate for every residential landlord to supply or maintain air conditioning; the statute instead lists requirements such as functioning heat in winter, running water, and hot water. Property owners and tenants review the lease and consult legal counsel when specific disputes arise. ### Common HVAC problems landlords face in Clearwater rental properties

Landlords in Clearwater face a consistent group of HVAC problems created by Florida’s climate, tenant usage patterns, and cycles of deferred maintenance. The 5 most common problems are **refrigerant leaks**, **clogged condensate drains**, **failed capacitors**, **dirty evaporator coils**, and **thermostat malfunctions**. **Refrigerant leaks** lower cooling capacity and make compressors run harder, reducing system lifespan by 3 to 7 years when left unrepaired. **Clogged condensate drains** cause water damage inside the equipment and activate float switch shutoffs, leaving tenants without cooling for 24 to 72 hours until service arrives. **Failed capacitors** stop motors from starting and rank among the most frequent single-component failures in Florida’s high-heat conditions. **Dirty evaporator coils** cut system efficiency by 20 to 30 percent and extend run cycles, raising tenant utility bills and speeding compressor wear. **Thermostat malfunctions** produce tenant complaints frequently mistaken for complete system breakdowns, which adds avoidable service call expenses. Landlords who skip annual maintenance usually experience higher volumes of emergency calls, and emergency repairs bring longer response times and greater labor costs than scheduled service visits.
